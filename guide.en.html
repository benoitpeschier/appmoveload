Handbook

Sensor, app, dashboard — the whole tour.

MoveLoad tracks canoe-kayak training load from a Movesense Flash sensor worn on the chest: heart rate and acceleration, read as intensity zones and compared against your own records. This guide covers both the athlete side (phone) and the coach side (web dashboard).

Installing MoveLoad — the app is distributed through TestFlight. You should receive an email invitation: install TestFlight from the App Store, open the invitation, then install MoveLoad from TestFlight. No invitation? Ask your coach.
Part 1

Athlete guide

Five tabs along the bottom of the app: Sessions, Sensor, Trends, HRV, Settings.

A session, from sensor to appIn this order — every outing

  1. 01

    Put the sensor on

    Clip the Movesense Flash onto the chest strap and put it on. There is no button: the sensor powers up on contact with the worn strap, and movement wakes it — tap or shake it if it does not respond.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Sensor tab → Scan. The app scans over Bluetooth for up to 15 seconds and connects automatically to the sensor it finds.

  3. 03

    Record

    Once connected: Start recording before going on the water, Stop recording when you come back. The sensor restarts and disconnects itself on stopping — that is expected.

  4. 04

    Fetch the session

    Reconnect (Scan), then Refresh to see the session under “Recordings on the sensor”, then Download. It is decoded and analysed automatically, and appears under Sessions.

i

Cannot connect? Usually the sensor has gone to sleep: off the strap it powers down and disappears from the scan entirely. Put it back on the strap and shake it. Otherwise check that Bluetooth is on and access granted (iOS Settings → MoveLoad → Bluetooth). Also worth knowing: a sensor already connected to another device stops advertising and becomes invisible — disconnect it from that device first.

The morning HRV testHRV tab — on waking, before you get up

Ten minutes exactly: five lying down, then five standing. What the test measures is how your nervous system reacts to the change of position, so the conditions matter as much as the measurement. Always do it the same way: on waking, before coffee, before your phone, before getting out of bed. A test taken under different conditions does not compare with the previous ones, and a series that does not compare is worth nothing.

  1. 01

    Get up, urinate, wet the strap

    On waking, get up and go to the toilet: a full bladder shifts the tone of the nervous system, which is exactly what the test measures. While you are there, wet the electrodes under the tap — a dry strap picks up nothing, and it is the number one reason a test will not start. Put the strap on before coming back.

  2. 02

    Lie back down, settle, start the test

    Lie down again on your back and let yourself come back down: you have just been walking, so the first minutes lying would be recovery rather than rest. The time it takes to open the app and settle is enough — do not count the seconds, do it the same way every morning. It is the consistency that makes the tests comparable, not the exact duration. Then the HRV tab → Start the test. The app connects and waits for the first heartbeat: the clock starts then, not when you tap. Put the phone down and stop looking at it. The screen will not switch itself off during the test.

  3. 03

    Five minutes lying down

    Still, on your back, without talking and without falling asleep. Breathe normally: do not try to slow your breathing down, it distorts the measurement. A chime and a vibration announce the end.

  4. 04

    Stand up, then five minutes standing

    Get up briskly at the chime: it is the abruptness of the change that produces the response being measured. Then stand still, leaning on nothing, until the second chime.

  5. 05

    Answer the five questions

    They come straight afterwards, on the same screen: fatigue, sleep, muscle soreness, stress, mood, each 1 to 5. Answer there and then — put off, they never get filled in. The coach sees only the total score, never your answers.

i

Why five minutes and not four. The first half-minute of each position and the last are left out of the calculation: the first is the transition rather than the position, the last is you anticipating the end. That leaves four analysed minutes per position, which is what the spectrum needs to be usable. It is also why a test cut short at four minutes is flagged as unreliable rather than thrown away.

!

What the test does not tell you. The verdict describes a physiological state and the deltas behind it. It never says which session to do: that depends on the week's plan, the water and how you feel, none of which the app knows. Nor does a single day mean anything — it is the repetition that speaks, which is why it is worth not skipping mornings.

The other screens

Sessions

Your full history. Swiping a session left deletes it — on the phone only; it stays on the sensor until the memory is erased.

Trends

How your records progress by window, perceived exertion over time, and time in the cardio and mechanical zones across all your sessions.

Manual import

A fallback: record with the Movesense Showcase app, export the two JSON files, import them from the Sensor tab.

!

Erasing the sensor's memory (Sensor tab) wipes the whole logbook at once — the sensor cannot delete one session on its own. The app warns you if some sessions have not been downloaded yet. Irreversible.

Part 2

Coach guide

A web dashboard, nothing to install — each athlete's data arrives live as soon as they sync from their phone.

Access

benoitpeschier.github.io/appmoveload

On first visit the team code is asked for — remember it, it acts as a shared password for the whole squad.

The Sign in button at the top right creates a coach account or signs you back into one, either with Continue with Google or with an e-mail address and a password. It is optional for now: the dashboard works without it. With an account you are recognised on any computer instead of depending on this one browser — and it is that account which will carry your role once teams arrive.

The project is set to one account per address: Google and a password on the same address will never create two separate coach accounts. That said, if you started with a password, Google sign-in on that address may be refused — Firebase asking you to prove the account is yours. If that happens, sign in with the password.

Navigation

Overview
One card per athlete — last session and summary. Click a card to open their detail page: a radar comparison over 8 mechanical windows against athletes in the same category (gender + boat), and the last session's curve against their own records.
Calendar
Month or Week view — spot the sessions on a time grid.
HRV tracking
The morning tests, one athlete per row. The trends use a logarithmic scale: a single outlier would flatten everything else on a linear one.
All · Training · Tests
Separates sessions marked “test” (standardised conditions, set by the athlete) from ordinary training — useful for comparing only what is genuinely comparable. The filter also applies to the records, the trend and the radar, not only to the list.

Reading an HRV testHRV tracking tab, morning banner, calendar pills

The test measures how the nervous system responds to going from lying to standing. Deltas are percentage changes against a reference (the median of the previous six tests, by default), and a cascade of five rules names the pattern: the first match wins, otherwise “no fatigue”. The thresholds come from the work of Laurent Schmidt and are those of the original workbook.

Morning banner
At the top of the overview, it names the athletes whose test today triggered a pattern. Those not in it triggered nothing — or did not test.
Heart pill on the calendar
Marks the days an athlete tested. The session's zone colours stay behind it, so yesterday's load and this morning's reading are on one screen.
Fatigue thresholds
In the drawer under each athlete's radar. Adjustable per athlete: these are benchmarks calibrated on a population, and an athlete may need different ones. You own them — the phone reads them and never writes them.
Delta on a small base
A huge percentage computed from a tiny value is flagged in amber. Going from 16 to 146 ms² is +800%, which does not mean the same thing as +800% from a normal base.
!

What you do not see, and why. The wellness questionnaire the athlete fills in after each test — fatigue, sleep, soreness, stress, mood — reaches you only as a total score. The five answers never leave their phone. That is the condition for answering it honestly, and an honest score beats five cautious answers. The verdict itself describes a physiological state: it never says which session to do.

i

Nothing to refresh by hand: the page updates as soon as an athlete syncs. The team code must not travel outside the squad.
